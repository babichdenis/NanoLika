# NanoLika 0.1.40 · Clear

🐉 **NanoLika `0.1.40` “Clear”**: the iPhone's input field is in the clear again, every account's chats are kept clearly apart on a shared device, every refusal from the relay is one clear sentence on every client, the Mac takes a true picture of the screen or says why it cannot, Windows starts again after the update that moved the app, and the operator of a relay can close the door with a switch.

Four releases reported a chat with no input field on the iPhone and iPad. It was there the whole time: behind the bottom bar, which was a safe-area inset that the chat stopped keeping clear of once the keyboard had come and gone. The bar is a row now, the composer is a row, and a page in Settings shows where the app laid it out, in a frame and in numbers. On Windows, 0.1.39 moved the app to a new folder and the profile's link to the plugin still pointed at the old one; the app starts over it now.

On the desktop every working light breathes at the phone's rhythm, the glow and the capsule while the hands work are the phone's, and the run's trajectory sits in the chat where the Live stage was. When NanoLika Cloud refuses a turn (the allowance spent, a message too large, a sign-in expired, a switch turned off by the operator) the desktop shows a card and the web app and the runtime say one sentence, never the relay's raw reply. On a Mac the helper takes the screen with ScreenCaptureKit, checks the Screen Recording grant truthfully and no longer substitutes a picture when it cannot take one. On the phones every chat belongs to an account, signing out asks one question, a refused key keeps the data and a deleted account removes it; the ChatGPT plan and your own providers get plain cards and a proxy setting. The relay's console has *Controls*, *Stats* and *Site* pages.

## Highlights

- **iPhone and iPad: the input field stays.** Four releases reported a chat with no input field; it was there, behind the bottom bar, a safe-area inset of the home screen that was hidden while the keyboard was up, and once it had left and come back the chat no longer kept clear of it: the field showed when the app opened and was gone for good after the first dismissed keyboard. The bar is now a row under the rooms, as on Android, and the composer column (the cards, the tool strip, the input bar) a row under the message list: plain layout, no overlay of the UIKit list, no second host to switch to. *Settings → Appearance → Composer check* draws a red frame around the column and writes a report (the window and its safe area, the column's frame, every UIKit ancestor with its frame, hidden flag and alpha, the watch's events) with *Copy* for a bug report: the view hierarchy a device can give without a Mac, in nine languages ([docs/ios.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/ios.md)).
- **Windows starts again after the update that moved the app.** 0.1.39 installed to `Programs\nanomuse-desktop` where 0.1.30 had been under `Programs\nanoMuse\…`; the profile's link to the plugin pointed at the old folder, and the launch died on `EEXIST: file already exists, symlink …`: the stale junction was being removed through a call that follows the link first and, finding nothing at its end, left it standing. The link is now removed as a link and remade; if that still fails, the message names the path to remove by hand ([docs/desktop.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/desktop.md)).
- **The desktop moves as the phone does.** Every light that said "working" (the chat and status dots, the other device's dot, the thinking dots, the microphone, the glow round the screen) breathes at the phone's rhythm (2.4 s in, 2.4 s out) instead of running or rippling, and is a steady light under the system's reduce-motion setting. The glow and the capsule while the hands work are the phone's: a light breathing along the four edges (blue working, amber waiting), the marker at the exact point of each click, a dashed path for a drag, and a pill at the top with the face, *Step N*, what the hands are doing, *I'll take it* and *Stop*. The picture-in-picture Live stage is gone; in its place the chat shows the trajectory of a hands run during and after it, each step's screenshot with the action drawn on it and the agent's words before it, with previous/next, *Open large* and *Copy this step* ([docs/gui.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/gui.md)).
- **What the relay refused, as a card or one sentence.** When nanoMuse Cloud refuses a turn the desktop's chat shows a card instead of the relay's reply (the allowance used up (the ways on and *Try again*), a message too large for the model (*New chat*), a sign-in that expired (*Sign in*), the relay busy) and a refusal is no longer retried five times as a rate limit before it shows. The cards know the operator's switches: the allowance paused rather than spent, the service, sync or hub paused, one sentence each. The web app and the hands runtime say the same in one plain sentence, in English and Chinese, instead of "The model provider answered with an error", and the ChatGPT plan's own failures (unreachable, region blocked, signed out, no quota, rate limited) get the same treatment. An own-key provider's refusal gets one sentence by kind with the reply folded under *What came back*, the ways-on block in Settings → nanoMuse Cloud reads the relay's guidance for where you are, and a one-line heads-up appears above the composer at 80 % of the allowance, as the phones show it. On both phones every refusal is one plain sentence in the phone's language with the right button, too large → *New chat*, a key the relay no longer takes → *Sign in*, a private relay or closed sign-ups → *Open Settings*, a busy provider with the wait, the operator's pauses → *Try again*, and when the operator pauses the allowance the card says paused, not used up; the ways on come from the relay's guidance for your region. The iPhone pins the allowance card under the chat header and shows the 80 % line too ([docs/parity.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/parity.md)).
- **macOS: a true picture of the screen, or the reason.** The helper *nanoMuse Computer Use* takes the screen with ScreenCaptureKit on macOS 14 and later (`CGDisplayCreateImage` returned nothing on macOS 26); 12.3 and 13 keep the CoreGraphics capture. The Screen Recording check asks ScreenCaptureKit, so the status says *granted*, *denied* or *unknown* truthfully and names the capture source. When the helper cannot take the picture the app no longer substitutes an Electron frame: the screenshot fails with the helper's own words, ScreenCaptureKit's error, the missing grant, or that the helper did not start. Window mode lists and captures windows through the helper too; a window it cannot capture parks the hands on the whole screen with the reason in the observation ([docs/desktop.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/desktop.md)).
- **Whose chats a phone shows.** Every chat on Android and iOS now belongs to the account that was signed in when it was made (or to nobody, while signed out), synced or not; the lists, the search, the home, the Library, the Siri shortcuts and the push show the signed-in account's only: a side chat or a pre-0.1.39 chat no longer shows under the next account (contract C12). Signing out asks *Keep this account's chats on this device* (off by default): off, the account's chats, memory, feed, goals, routines and face leave the phone; on, they are put aside and come back at the next sign-in; signing in as another account and *Change server* ask the same. A key the relay refuses keeps the data on the phone, put aside until the same account signs in again; only a relay answer that the account was deleted removes it: the relay remembers a deleted account's keys for 90 days for exactly that answer, and *Delete the account* removes everything of it on the phone with no question. A fresh install starts empty on both phones: Android no longer takes part in the device backup, iOS keeps the relay's key out of iCloud Keychain and sweeps what a previous install left ([docs/sync.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/sync.md)).
- **The ChatGPT plan and your own providers, on the phones.** When chatgpt.com or an own-key provider cannot be reached, when OpenAI does not serve the region, when the ChatGPT sign-in has expired or the plan has nothing left in the window, both phones show a plain card that says what helps, with *Try again*, instead of the socket text. *Settings → Network → HTTP proxy for own providers* (host, port, optional user and password; off by default; this device only) is used for own-key providers and the ChatGPT plan, never for nanoMuse Cloud, with a *Test* row. The allowance card lists every way on (wait, your own key, a model of your own, your computer, invite) and the daily cap gets its own line. On the iPhone *Use your own model key* lists the whole catalogue (the region's providers first, what each key covers, *Get a key*, the key taken on the vendor's own sheet) and the plans you already pay for: ChatGPT (with the line about OpenAI's terms), Claude, Kimi, OpenRouter; a server on a computer of your own (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) takes its address ([docs/own-key.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)).
- **The operator's console: Controls, Stats, Site.** *Controls* has switches for the free allowance, sign-ups, the cloud service, conversation sync and the device hub, applied at once and kept across restarts, each saying what turning it off does; threshold rules that close sign-ups, pause the allowance or sync, or send a notice when the account count reaches a number; and an audit log of who changed what and when (`nanomuse-cloud admin controls list|set|audit` from the shell). *Stats* shows accounts, devices, model calls and tokens, allowance use, sync volume, errors and API calls per UTC day and by category, each with a note on how it is computed, as a table and a CSV. *Site* shows the repository's stars, forks, watchers, open issues and release downloads per day, read from GitHub every six hours or on demand. The console shows accounts' phone numbers and e-mail addresses in full; logs, e-mails and public pages keep the masked hint ([docs/cloud.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/cloud.md)).

## Upgrade Notes

- **Android installs over 0.1.39 and keeps your data.** `NanoLika-0.1.40-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0+, arm64; versionCode 41) is signed with the same key as every version before it. `allowBackup` is now off: nothing goes to the device backup, so a reinstall starts empty and the account's chats come back through sync. Verify with `sha256sum -c NanoLika-0.1.40-arm64.apk.sha256`.
- **The relay is 0.22.** New refusal codes (`403 signup_closed`, `503 service_paused`, `503 sync_paused`, `503 hub_paused`, `allowance_exhausted` with `paused: true`) and `paused` in `/v1/config` and `/v1/me`; the *Controls*, *Stats* and *Site* pages; `401 account_deleted` from a 90-day tombstone of a deleted account's keys. 0.21 clients ignore the new fields; 0.1.40 clients on a 0.21 relay see the old sentences. Deploy the relay first: `cloud/deploy/…/deploy.sh` or `docker compose up -d --build`.
- **macOS 14 and later:** grant Screen Recording to *NanoLika Computer Use* (the helper's own name in the pane) and the app no longer substitutes a frame when the grant is missing; the hands say what to switch on. Ad-hoc signed still: a new build starts the grants over.
- **Windows:** install 0.1.40 over 0.1.39; the stale link from the moved install is remade on the first start, and if it cannot be, the message names the path to remove by hand.
- **iPhone and iPad:** build 13 on TestFlight; the public link follows Apple's beta review. Signing out is a sheet now, with the question about this account's chats.
- **The desktop installers are not notarised yet.** On macOS open the app once from *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (or right-click → *Open*); on Windows click *Run anyway*. The checksum sits next to every file.

## Community

nanoMuse is open source and free. Sign in with a phone number or an e-mail and you get a starting allowance; the developer pays for it. The account page shows what is left and how to add more. When it is gone, use your own key or a plan you already pay for ([how](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)). With an account, the text of your main conversation is kept on nanoMuse Cloud so that your devices show the same chat (side chats only if you say so) and one switch in Data controls turns it off and deletes it; nothing is sold ([privacy policy](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/)); delete the account whenever you want. The same relay runs on a server of yours: [docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md).

This is a preview. We use it every day and know where it is rough; tell us where it broke for you and what you want it to do. ### 亮点

- **iPhone 和 iPad：输入框留得住。** 四个版本都有人报告聊天没有输入框；它一直在，在底栏后面。底栏是首页的一块安全区内边距，键盘弹起时隐藏，它退下去再回来之后聊天就不再给它让位：输入框在应用打开时能看到，第一次收起键盘之后就再也不见。现在底栏是房间下面的一行，和 Android 一样；输入栏这一列（卡片、工具条、输入框）是消息列表下面的一行，普通布局，不盖在 UIKit 列表上，没有第二个宿主可切换。「设置 → 外观 → 输入框检查」在这一列四周画一个红框，并写一份报告，窗口和它的安全区、这一列的位置和尺寸、每一个 UIKit 祖先的位置、隐藏标记和透明度、看门狗的事件，有「复制」键，直接贴进 issue：不用 Mac 也拿得到的视图层级，九种语言（[docs/ios.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/ios.md)）。
- **Windows：挪了位置的更新之后还能启动。** 0.1.39 装到了 `Programs\nanomuse-desktop`，0.1.30 原来在 `Programs\nanoMuse\…`；配置目录里指向插件的链接还指着旧文件夹，启动时死在 `EEXIST: file already exists, symlink …`，过期的 junction 原来是用一个先顺着链接走的调用删的，链接尽头什么都没有，它就把链接留在了原地。现在链接按链接删掉再重建；还是不行的话，提示会写明要手动删除的路径（[docs/desktop.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/desktop.md)）。
- **桌面端的动静和手机一样。** 每一盏表示「工作中」的灯（聊天和状态的圆点、另一台设备的圆点、思考中的点、麦克风、屏幕四周的光晕），都按手机的节奏呼吸（吸 2.4 秒、呼 2.4 秒），不再跑动或泛起涟漪；系统开了「减弱动态效果」就是一盏稳定的灯。「手」干活时的光晕和胶囊是手机那一套：四边呼吸的光（蓝色在做、琥珀色在等），每次点击的准确位置上有标记，拖动是一条带箭头的虚线，屏幕顶上一粒胶囊，带着脸、「第 N 步」、「手」正在做什么、「我来接手」和「停止」。画中画的 Live 舞台没有了；聊天里改为显示一次运行的轨迹，运行中和运行后都能看，每一步的截图画上动作、这一步之前智能体说的话，有上一步/下一步、「放大」和「复制这一步」（[docs/gui.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/gui.md)）。
- **中继拒绝了什么，一张卡片或一句话。** nanoMuse Cloud 拒绝一轮对话时，桌面端的聊天显示一张卡片而不是中继的回复，额度用完（往下走的几条路和「重试」）、消息超出模型窗口（「新聊天」）、登录过期（「登录」）、中继忙，拒绝也不再先当成限流重试五次才显示。卡片认得运维者的开关：额度是暂停而不是用完、服务、同步或设备通道暂停，各一句话。网页应用和「手」的运行时用同样的一句直白的话说明，中英文都有，不再是「模型服务商返回了错误」；ChatGPT 套餐自己的故障（连不上、地区不支持、登录过期、额度用完、限流）也同样处理。自己的 key 被服务商拒绝时按故障种类给一句话，服务商的回复折在「返回了什么」里；「设置 → nanoMuse Cloud」里的「往下走的路」按你所在的地区读中继的引导；额度用到 80% 时输入框上方有一行提醒，和手机上一样。两部手机上，中继的每一次拒绝都是手机语言的一句直白的话加上对的按钮，消息太长 →「新聊天」、中继不再接受的 key →「登录」、私有中继或已关闭的注册 →「打开设置」、服务商忙则带上等待时间、运维者的暂停 →「重试」，运维者暂停额度时卡片说的是「暂停」而不是「用完」；往下走的路来自中继对你所在地区的引导。iPhone 把额度卡片固定在聊天标题栏下方，也有 80% 那一行（[docs/parity.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/parity.md)）。
- **macOS：屏幕的真实画面，或者原因。** 助手「nanoMuse Computer Use」在 macOS 14 及以后用 ScreenCaptureKit 截屏（macOS 26 上 `CGDisplayCreateImage` 什么也不返回）；12.3 和 13 仍用 CoreGraphics。屏幕录制权限的检查直接问 ScreenCaptureKit，状态如实报告「已授权」「已拒绝」或「未知」，并写明截图来源。助手拍不到时，应用不再拿 Electron 的画面顶替：截图失败，原因是助手自己的话，ScreenCaptureKit 的错误、缺少屏幕录制授权、或者助手没有启动。窗口模式也通过助手列出并截取窗口；截不到的窗口把「手」停在整个屏幕上，原因写进观察里（[docs/desktop.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/desktop.md)）。
- **一部手机显示谁的聊天。** Android 和 iOS 上每条聊天现在都归创建它时登录的账号（退出登录时创建的归无人），同步与否都一样；列表、搜索、首页、资料库、Siri 快捷指令和推送只显示当前登录账号的，旁聊或 0.1.39 之前的聊天不再出现在下一个账号名下（契约 C12）。退出登录会问「把这个账号的聊天留在这台设备上」（默认关）：关，这个账号的聊天、记忆、动态、目标、例行和形象离开手机；开，它们被收起来，下次登录时回来；换另一个账号登录和「更换服务器」问同一个问题。中继拒绝的 key 不会删数据：数据收在手机上，等同一个账号再登录就恢复；只有中继回答「账号已删除」才会清除，中继为此把已删除账号的 key 记 90 天；「删除账号」则不问，手机上属于它的全部清掉。全新安装两部手机都从空白开始：Android 不再参与设备备份，iOS 把中继的 key 留在本机、不进 iCloud 钥匙串，并清掉上一次安装留下的东西（[docs/sync.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/sync.md)）。
- **手机上的 ChatGPT 套餐和自己的服务商。** chatgpt.com 或自己的服务商连不上、OpenAI 不服务所在地区、ChatGPT 登录过期、套餐在这个窗口里没有余量时，两部手机都显示一张直白的卡片说明怎么办，带「重试」，不再是 socket 的原文。「设置 → 网络 → 自有服务商的 HTTP 代理」（主机、端口、可选的用户名和密码；默认关；仅本设备）用于自己的 key 和 ChatGPT 套餐，绝不用于 nanoMuse Cloud，有一行「测试」。额度卡片列出每一条路（等、自己的 key、自己的模型、自己的电脑、邀请），每日上限单独一行。iPhone 上「换成自己的 key」列出完整目录，所在地区的服务商在前、每把 key 能做什么、「获取 key」、在服务商自己的页面里填 key，以及你已经在付费的套餐：ChatGPT（附 OpenAI 条款的那句说明）、Claude、Kimi、OpenRouter；自己电脑上的服务（Ollama、LM Studio、vLLM）填地址即可（[docs/own-key.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)）。
- **运维者的控制台：控制、统计、官网。** 「控制」页有免费额度、注册、云服务、对话同步和设备通道五个开关，立即生效、重启后保留，每个都写明关掉意味着什么；阈值规则在账号数到达某个数字时关闭注册、暂停额度或同步、或发一封通知；还有一份审计日志，记录谁在什么时候改了什么（命令行是 `nanomuse-cloud admin controls list|set|audit`）。「统计」页按 UTC 日和类别给出账号、设备、模型调用和 token、额度使用、同步量、错误和 API 调用，每一项附一句怎么算的，有表格和 CSV。「官网」页是仓库的 Star、Fork、Watch、未关闭的 issue 和每天的发布下载量，每六小时从 GitHub 读一次，也可以手动刷新。控制台里账号的手机号和邮箱完整显示；日志、邮件和公开页面仍是打码的（[docs/cloud.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/cloud.md)）。

### 升级说明

- **Android 覆盖安装 0.1.39，数据保留。** `NanoLika-0.1.40-arm64.apk`（Android 8.0+，arm64；versionCode 41）和之前每个版本用同一把签名。`allowBackup` 现在是关的：不再进设备备份，所以卸载重装从空白开始，账号的聊天通过同步回来。校验：`sha256sum -c NanoLika-0.1.40-arm64.apk.sha256`。
- **中继是 0.22。** 新的拒绝码（`403 signup_closed`、`503 service_paused`、`503 sync_paused`、`503 hub_paused`、带 `paused: true` 的 `allowance_exhausted`），以及 `/v1/config` 和 `/v1/me` 里的 `paused`；「控制」「统计」「官网」三页；已删除账号的 key 记 90 天，回答 `401 account_deleted`。0.21 的客户端忽略新字段；0.1.40 的客户端对着 0.21 中继看到的是旧的句子。先部署中继：`cloud/deploy/…/deploy.sh` 或 `docker compose up -d --build`。
- **macOS 14 及以后：** 把屏幕录制授权给「NanoLika Computer Use」，权限面板里是助手自己的名字；缺少授权时应用不再拿别的画面顶替，「手」会说要打开什么。仍是 ad-hoc 签名：新构建要重新授权。
- **Windows：** 用 0.1.40 覆盖安装 0.1.39；挪位置留下的过期链接在第一次启动时重建，重建不了，提示会写明要手动删除的路径。
- **iPhone 和 iPad：** TestFlight 上的构建 13；公测链接等 Apple 的 beta 审核。退出登录现在是一张弹出页，带着关于这个账号的聊天的那个问题。
- **桌面安装包还没有公证。** macOS 第一次在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里点「仍要打开」（或右键 → 「打开」），Windows 点「仍要运行」。每个文件旁边都有校验值。

### 社区

nanoMuse 开源、免费。手机号或邮箱登录后有一份起始额度，钱是开发者出的；剩多少、怎么加，账号页里有数。用完可以换成自己的 key，或者用你已经在付费的套餐登录（[教程](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)）。登录后，主聊天的文字会保存在 nanoMuse Cloud，让你的几台设备看到同样的对话（旁聊只在你说要的时候才上去）「数据控制」里一个开关就能关掉并删除；数据不卖（[隐私政策](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/)）；账号想删就删。同一个中继也能跑在你自己的服务器上：[docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md)。
