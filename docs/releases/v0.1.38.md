# NanoLika 0.1.38 · Loom

🐉 **NanoLika `0.1.38` “Loom”** is the thread from 0.1.37 made to carry its weight. Side chats stay on the device that made them unless you say otherwise, so a device signing in gets the main conversation's newest three hundred lines in one pull instead of the account's whole history; a device at work says so under its line on the others, instead of leaving a prompt that looks interrupted; and the computer-use turn that failed with *413 Request too large* cannot any more: the hands keep at most four screenshots of two megapixels in a request on every client, and the relay accepts sixteen megabytes. The desktop no longer re-reads every session log every two seconds while idle, which is where the slowness of 0.1.37 came from.

The hands were taken apart on two systems. On a Mac they have a helper app of their own, **NanoLika Computer Use**, the way Codex does it: the Screen Recording and Accessibility grants belong to the helper, the panes list it by that name, and switching Screen Recording on no longer needs the app restarted. On Ubuntu the hands were run on this machine, under a nested X server, and fixed where they failed: the glow around the screen was swallowing every click, Shift was dropped for symbols, and an approved step came back *Not done* because the app and the runtime signed the approval ticket differently.

The iPhone's composer is a native text field now, with a second place to stand if the first is taken away; the launch screen is the mark, not the words of the app we are built on. Every client can point at a relay of yours, and `scripts/self-host.sh` brings one up. The README is half as long, `AGENTS.md` says how to work in the tree, the docs are a site at [nanomuse.cn/docs](https://nanomuse.cn/docs/), and the roadmap says where a contributor can start.

## Highlights

- **Main first.** Only the main conversation syncs by default; side chats stay on the device that made them. *Also sync side chats* in Data controls, per device, sends this device's side chats to the account and brings the others' here. The first pull after sign-in is the newest 300 messages (`tail=300`), so a new device shows the conversation at once; the relay's `/v1/sync/changes` takes `scope` and `tail` (Cloud 0.20).
- **{device} is working…** When another device is answering a prompt, the line says so under it for up to ten minutes (`POST /v1/sync/working`, a `working` hub frame); on Android the *已中断 · 继续* banner no longer appears under a prompt another device is handling, on every client a line written elsewhere never shows interrupted/continue.
- **No more 413.** The relay accepts 16 MiB (was 6) and says both sizes when it refuses; the runtime keeps the newest four screenshots at ≤ 2 Mpx in a request and replaces the older ones with a note; the desktop's harness carries the same image budget in its provider row; Android elides tool-result images beyond four; the desktop operator's screenshots are capped at 2 Mpx on every path.
- **The desktop is quick again.** A push with nothing new re-marked every session dirty and ran again two seconds later, reading every session log each time: ~50 % of a core on a large account, a wait behind every click. Idle CPU is 0.1 % now and the UI's p95 went from 60–80 ms to 4 ms on a 33-session account. Session titles come from the harness's projection, a log is read once per change, hub frames arriving together become one pull.
- **macOS: NanoLika Computer Use.** A Swift helper in `Contents/Helpers` takes the screenshots (`CGDisplayCreateImage`) and posts the mouse and keyboard events (`CGEvent`, text of any script typed as characters). The permission panes list it instead of the app; the app restarts it by itself when a grant changes. Without the helper bundle everything works as in 0.1.37. Still ad-hoc signed, so a new build starts the grants over until the Developer ID certificate exists: `docs/desktop.md` says how to reset them.
- **Ubuntu: the hands work.** Clicks and keystrokes land (the glow's X11 input shape is re-applied after it maps and after each action); `*`, `@`, `()` come out right (libnut dropped Shift); *Allow once* and *Always allow* run the step (the approval ticket now verifies); a Wayland session is told plainly that the hands are off, in Settings and in the chat, instead of a black picture; HiDPI roots click in root pixels. Tested in Xephyr with a calculator, gedit, approvals, Stop and the first-run page.
- **iPhone: the composer, for real.** The field is a SwiftUI `TextField`; the UIKit host iOS left empty when the naming card went away is gone with it, and a fail-safe shows the composer from a second attachment point if it still reports no height a second after the chat appears or a message goes out. The launch screen is the mark on the system background.
- **A relay of yours, from every app.** *Use a different server* on the sign-in page of Android and iOS (address → *Check* → *Use this server*; `http://` for private addresses only), *Server: host / Change* in Account; the desktop and the runtime through their configuration. `scripts/self-host.sh` asks five questions and brings up a relay with TLS (`--local` for a loopback one), proven end to end on this machine.
- **For contributors.** README in 110 lines (and nine translations in the same shape), `AGENTS.md` with the layout, the checks and the conventions, a roadmap that says what we are catching up with Muse on and where to start, and the docs as a VitePress site.
- **Sign-in outside mainland China.** Every client says, before a code is requested, that text-message codes reach mainland-China numbers only and to use an e-mail address; Android has 18 new strings in 17 languages, iOS 17 in nine.

## Upgrade Notes

- **Android installs over 0.1.37 and keeps your data.** `NanoLika-0.1.38-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0+, arm64; versionCode 39) is signed with the same key as every version before it. Verify with `sha256sum -c NanoLika-0.1.38-arm64.apk.sha256`.
- **The relay is 0.20.** `/v1/sync/changes?scope&tail`, `POST /v1/sync/working`, `working` in `/v1/sync/state`, `MAX_REQUEST_BYTES` 16 MiB. Clients of 0.1.38 still work against a 0.19 relay, but the main-first pull and the working line need 0.20; a self-hosted relay: `cloud/deploy/…/deploy.sh` or `docker compose up -d --build`. If a proxy sits in front of your relay, let it pass 20 MB bodies.
- **Side chats stop syncing by default.** A device that had side chats on the account keeps the ones it has; new side-chat turns stay on the device until *Also sync side chats* is on there. Turn it on on the devices you want them on, each device decides for itself.
- **macOS: grant the permissions once more, to the helper.** System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording and Accessibility now list *NanoLika Computer Use*; the rows for NanoLika Desktop can be removed. The build is still ad-hoc signed: a later build asks again. `tccutil reset ScreenCapture io.github.nanomuse.desktop.computer-use` resets.
- **Linux is X11 only** for the hands; a Wayland session says so and points at an Xorg session. Nothing to grant on X11.
- **iOS is on TestFlight**, internal testers for now; the public link follows Apple's beta review. The launch screen changes after a reinstall (iOS caches it per install).
- **The desktop installers are not notarised yet.** On macOS open the app once from *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (or right-click → *Open*); on Windows click *Run anyway*. The checksum sits next to every file.

## Community

nanoMuse is open source and free. Sign in with a phone number or an e-mail and you get a starting allowance; the developer pays for it. The account page shows what is left and how to add more. When it is gone, use your own key: Alibaba Cloud Bailian in mainland China, OpenRouter elsewhere ([how](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)). With an account, the text of your main conversation is kept on nanoMuse Cloud so that your devices show the same chat (side chats only if you say so) and one switch in Data controls turns it off and deletes it; nothing is sold ([privacy policy](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/)); delete the account whenever you want. The same relay runs on a server of yours: [docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md).

This is a preview. We use it every day and know where it is rough; tell us where it broke for you and what you want it to do. 「手」在两个系统上拆开检查了一遍。Mac 上，它们有了自己的助手应用 **NanoLika Computer Use**，照 Codex 的做法：屏幕录制和辅助功能的授权属于助手，权限面板里列的是它的名字，打开屏幕录制后不用再重启应用。Ubuntu 上，在这台机器的嵌套 X 服务器里把「手」跑了一遍，坏的地方修了：屏幕四周的光晕吞掉了每一次点击、符号丢了 Shift、批准过的步骤回来是「Not done」，因为应用和运行时对审批票据的签法不一样。

iPhone 的输入框换成原生文本框，第一个落脚点被拿走时还有第二个；启动页是标志，不再是我们所基于的那个应用的字样。每个客户端都能指向你自己的中继，`scripts/self-host.sh` 能把它搭起来。README 短了一半，`AGENTS.md` 写了在这棵树里怎么干活，文档成了一个站点 [nanomuse.cn/docs](https://nanomuse.cn/docs/)，路线图写了想加入的人从哪儿入手。

### 亮点

- **主聊天优先。** 默认只同步主聊天，旁聊留在写它的设备上。「数据控制」里的「同时同步旁聊」按设备设置：开了，本机的旁聊上传到账号、别的设备的旁聊同步过来。登录后第一次拉取是最新 300 条（`tail=300`），新设备马上能看到对话；中继的 `/v1/sync/changes` 多了 `scope` 和 `tail`（Cloud 0.20）。
- **「{设备} 正在处理…」** 别的设备在回答一条消息时，那条消息下面会这样显示，最多十分钟（`POST /v1/sync/working`，hub 的 `working` 帧）；Android 上「已中断 · 继续」不再出现在别的设备正在处理的消息下面，各端对别处写的消息都不再显示已中断/继续。
- **413 没有了。** 中继接受 16 MiB（原来 6），拒绝时把两个数字都说出来；运行时一次请求只保留最新四张截图、每张不超过 2 Mpx，更早的换成一行说明；桌面端 harness 的 provider 行带同样的图片预算；Android 把第四张以后的工具结果图片省略；桌面操作器每条路径的截图都封在 2 Mpx。
- **桌面端又快了。** 一次没有新内容的推送把所有会话重新标脏、两秒后再跑一遍，每次都重读全部会话日志，大账号上占掉半个核，每次点击都要等。现在空闲 CPU 0.1 %，33 个会话的账号上 UI p95 从 60–80 ms 降到 4 ms。会话标题取自 harness 的投影，一份日志每次变化只读一遍，一起到的 hub 帧合成一次拉取。
- **macOS：NanoLika Computer Use。** `Contents/Helpers` 里的一个 Swift 助手负责截图（`CGDisplayCreateImage`）和鼠标键盘事件（`CGEvent`，任何文字按字符输入）。权限面板列的是它而不是应用；授权变化时应用自己重启它。没有助手包时一切同 0.1.37。仍是 ad-hoc 签名，所以在 Developer ID 证书办下来之前，新构建仍要重新授权，`docs/desktop.md` 写了怎么重置。
- **Ubuntu：「手」能用了。** 点击和按键落得到位置（光晕的 X11 输入形状在映射后和每次动作后重新设置）；`*`、`@`、`()` 打得对（libnut 丢了 Shift）；「允许一次」和「始终允许」真的会执行那一步（审批票据现在能验过）；Wayland 会话在设置和聊天里都明白地说「手」关着，而不是给一张黑图；HiDPI 下按根窗口像素点击。在 Xephyr 里用计算器、gedit、审批、停止和首次运行页测过。
- **iPhone：输入框，这次是真的。** 文本框是 SwiftUI 的 `TextField`；起名卡片消失时被 iOS 清空的那个 UIKit 宿主跟着没了；如果聊天出现或消息发出一秒后它仍报告零高度，一个兜底会从第二个挂载点把输入框显示出来。启动页是系统底色上的标志。
- **从每个应用指向你自己的中继。** Android 和 iOS 登录页上的「使用其他服务器」（地址 → 检查 → 使用这个服务器；只有内网地址允许 `http://`），账号页里的「服务器：host / 更改」；桌面端和运行时走配置。`scripts/self-host.sh` 问五个问题就把一台带 TLS 的中继搭起来（`--local` 是回环地址的），在这台机器上端到端验证过。
