# NanoLika 0.1.33 · Steps

🐉 **nanoMuse `0.1.33` “Steps”** is about the words under the face. On every client the line under the agent's name now says what the running step *is* in the model's own words (*打开携程网站*, *Check the login page*) not *Running: `<command>`*: every tool takes a `step` argument, stripped before the tool runs, shown under the face and as the pill's title. The iPhone gets the Muse shell (the face, that status line, the drawer, the rooms, the avatar studio, the connectors, data controls, Reach) and so stands with the Android phone, the desktop and the web app. What is still open for a decision is written down in [docs/parity.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/parity.md).

Three of this version's fixes came out of using the desktop for a day. A click on the face in the profile drawer looked like nothing: its menu was being drawn below the window. The app forgot its preferences on every launch: the host's port changed each time, and the port is the key to everything the browser side keeps. And the hands' screenshots came back black on a Mac without Screen Recording granted to the fresh process, and went to the model as if they were the screen. Each is fixed, and each is the kind of thing a report with a screenshot finds faster than any test; keep sending them.

## Highlights

- **The step's own words, under the face and on the pill.** The runtime asks the model for `step` on every tool call, in the person's language; `nanomuse mcp` carries it to the desktop's hands; Android keeps the running step's title between steps; the web app's pills show it. The status line stops reading like a terminal.
- **The iPhone's Muse shell.** `NanoMuseRoot` takes the root on the iPhone (the iPad keeps upstream's split layout): the chat's title is the face, the name and the status line, *waiting for you*, the step's words, *writing the reply*, *On it: …*, the model's name when idle, with the face breathing and popping in its five moods. The bottom bar carries the rooms: Ideas and Library are real, Feed and Goals are empty states until the phone has something to run them with. The avatar studio draws through the relay and pushes the look to the account; the connectors catalogue (75 services) with the client-id ask; Data controls; Reach to the other devices; the star asks. Three upstream edits, each marked.
- **The browser is handed over, not described (Android).** A page that needs the person (a login, a code, a payment, a CAPTCHA) used to end the turn with *please log in yourself*. `browser_use` has a `hand_over` action: the agent's own tab opens for the person with its session, a **Your turn** card says what the page asks with *Open the page* and *Done, continue*, and the agent waits and goes on from the page as it is, told never to ask for or type credentials. Hands approvals are answered on the floating capsule (**Allow / Deny**) without coming back into the app (money still asks in the chat card with the screen lock).
- **Connectors that need an app of your own say so.** Eight services (GitHub, Slack, Discord, HubSpot, Render, Bitrise, PagerDuty, Box) register no clients by themselves, and *Connect* used to fail there with one line. On the desktop, Android and iOS the sheet now walks through making an OAuth app at the vendor's developer page with our callback address (shown, copyable) and pasting its client id; the id is kept like a registration. Six services join the catalogue (Attio, GitLab, Miro, QuickBooks, YNAB, Oura: 75). On Android, Connectors and MCP are one Settings entry, with *Your own servers* at the end.
- **The desktop, after a day's use.** The face's menu is where the face is; the host's port is kept across launches so the preferences, the star asks' memory and the stage's place survive; the trace view has a way out (a *Chat · Trajectory* pill at the top right, Esc); theme swatches are gone (the accent follows the avatar, as everywhere); the typing dots are centred; permissions read back the moment you return from System Settings, *Allow* becomes *Open System Settings* once pressed, and Screen Recording granted while the app runs gets a notice with *Restart now* (macOS applies it only to freshly started apps); the live stage moves and resizes and remembers where it was; the preset's own routes are admitted like the harness's `/api`.
- **A black screenshot is an error, not a picture.** A capture that comes back all black (macOS without Screen Recording for this process, Wayland on Linux) raises `BlackScreen` with what to do, and no black frame reaches the model or the stage.
- **`?token=` is refused.** Announced in 0.1.31, kept one more version in 0.1.32: a token in a query string is a 401 that says so; a socket that opens with one gets `legacy_token` and a 4401 close.
- **Also in this version.** Star asks with a thank-you's tone at the tenth task and after a new look, on every client; a run the desktop host started on its own (the first meeting) is not counted as the person's task; the avatar studio says *sign in first* in the person's language; NanoLika Cloud 0.16 sets a pool to any figure, one account or all (`/v1/admin/pool`, `/batch`), already in production.

## Upgrade Notes

- **Android installs over 0.1.32 and keeps your data.** `NanoLika-0.1.33-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0+, arm64; versionCode 34) is signed with the same key as every version before it. Verify with `sha256sum -c NanoLika-0.1.33-arm64.apk.sha256`.
- **The desktop remembers from this version on.** The first launch of 0.1.33 picks a port and writes it to `~/.nanomuse/desktop/port`; what 0.1.32 kept in the browser's storage under its random port of the day is not carried over: the onboarding slides and the star asks may show once more.
- **`?token=` links no longer work** against a 0.1.33 runtime. The web app and the phone have used first-frame auth and `#token=` since 0.1.31; a script that still appends `?token=` gets a 401 whose body says what to change.
- **Connectors.** The client-id path and the browser hand-over compile and follow the desktop's flow, but neither has been signed into or handed over on a device by the maintainer yet (no emulator on the build machine): a report of a service that does not complete, with its vendor, is the most useful thing to send.
- **iOS is TestFlight-internal.** The Muse shell is on the iPhone only; the iPad keeps upstream's layout. No public link yet.
- **Running a relay?** 0.16.0 adds `POST /v1/admin/pool` and `/v1/admin/pool/batch` with the events `pool.set` / `pool.set.many`; no migration.
- **The desktop installers are not notarised.** On macOS open the app once from *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (or right-click → *Open*); on Windows click *Run anyway*. The checksum sits next to every file.

## Community

**Free, open source, non-profit: open source, built together: a personal agent for all.** Sign in with a phone number or an e-mail and the model comes with a free allowance, paid by the developer, the account page says how much is left and how to go on when it is spent (your own key, a friend's invitation). If NanoLika has been useful, a star on GitHub is the one thing we ask for: your support is what keeps us going.

This is a preview, and the people building it use it every day; what breaks for you, and what you wish it did, is the most useful thing you can send. What we are after is bigger than one app: a personal agent that belongs to the person who runs it, on every device they own, open to anyone who wants to build on it.

🐉 **nanoMuse `0.1.33`「Steps」**：这一版说的是头像下面那行字。现在每个端上，智能体名字下面的那一行说的是正在进行的这一步*本身*，「打开携程网站」「检查登录页」，用的是模型自己的话，而不是「正在执行：`<命令>`」：每个工具都多了一个 `step` 参数，在工具运行前被剥掉，显示在头像下方，也作为胶囊的标题。iPhone 有了 Muse 外壳（表情、这行状态、抽屉、房间、形象工坊、连接器、数据控制、Reach），从此和 Android 手机、桌面端、网页版站在一起。还没定的事写在 [docs/parity.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/parity.md)。

这一版有三个修复来自把桌面端用了一天。点抽屉里的头像像是没反应，它的菜单被画到了窗口下面。应用每次启动都忘掉设置，宿主端口每次都变，而端口正是浏览器那一侧所有存储的钥匙。还有，Mac 上没给新进程授权屏幕录制时，手的截图回来是全黑的，却照样当作屏幕发给模型。三个都修了；而每一个都是一张带截图的报告比任何测试找得更快的那种，请继续发来。

### 亮点

- **这一步自己的话，在头像下、在胶囊上。** 运行时在每次工具调用时都向模型要 `step`，用对方的语言；`nanomuse mcp` 把它带给桌面端的手；Android 在步骤之间保留正在进行的那一步的标题；网页版的胶囊也显示它。状态行不再像终端。
- **iPhone 的 Muse 外壳。** `NanoMuseRoot` 在 iPhone 上接管根视图（iPad 保留上游的分栏布局）：聊天标题变成表情、名字和状态行，「等你回答」、这一步的话、「正在写回复」、「正在处理：…」、空闲时是模型名，表情在五种情绪里呼吸、起伏、弹跳。底栏是房间：想法和资料库是真的，动态和目标先是空状态，等手机有东西能跑它们再说。形象工坊通过中继作画，并把形象推到账号；连接器目录（75 个服务）带客户端 ID 的询问；数据控制；到其他设备的 Reach；star 提示。改了三处上游代码，每处都有标记。
- **浏览器交给你，而不是告诉你（Android）。** 需要本人的页面（登录、验证码、付款、人机验证），以前会以一句「请你自己登录」结束这一轮。`browser_use` 多了 `hand_over` 动作：智能体自己的标签页带着会话打开给你，一张「轮到你了」卡片说明页面需要什么，带「打开页面」和「完成，继续」，智能体等着，然后从页面当下的样子接着做，并被告知永远不要索要或输入凭据。手的审批在悬浮胶囊上直接回答（**允许 / 拒绝**），不用回到应用（涉及金钱的仍在聊天卡片里确认，会要求验证锁屏）。
- **需要你自己建应用的连接器会说出来。** 有八个服务，GitHub、Slack、Discord、HubSpot、Render、Bitrise、PagerDuty、Box，不会自己注册客户端，以前在那里点「连接」只会失败并给一行错误。现在桌面端、Android 和 iOS 的面板会一步步带你到厂商的开发者页面建一个 OAuth 应用，填上我们的回调地址（显示出来，可复制），再粘贴它的客户端 ID；这个 ID 像注册一样保存。目录新增六个服务（Attio、GitLab、Miro、QuickBooks、YNAB、Oura，共 75 个）。Android 上连接器和 MCP 合成设置里的一个入口，末尾是「你自己的服务器」。
- **桌面端，用了一天之后。** 头像的菜单在头像该在的地方；宿主端口跨启动保持，设置、star 提示的记忆、舞台的位置都留得住；轨迹视图有了出口（右上角的「对话 · 轨迹」胶囊，Esc）；主题色块去掉了（强调色跟随头像，各端一致）；输入中的三个点居中了；从系统设置回来的一刻权限状态就刷新，「允许」按过一次后变成「打开系统设置」，应用运行中授予的屏幕录制会提示「立即重启」（macOS 只对重新启动的应用生效）；舞台可以拖动、缩放并记住位置；插件自己的路由和 Harness 的 `/api` 一样经过准入。
- **全黑的截图是错误，不是图片。** 回来全黑的一帧（Mac 上这个进程没有屏幕录制权限、Linux 上的 Wayland），会抛出 `BlackScreen` 并说明怎么办，不会有黑帧到达模型或舞台。
- **`?token=` 被拒绝。** 0.1.31 预告、0.1.32 多留了一版：查询串里的令牌现在返回说明原因的 401；带着它打开的套接字收到 `legacy_token` 和 4401 关闭。
- **这一版还有。** 每个端在第十个任务后和换了新形象后，用致谢的语气请你点 star；桌面宿主自己发起的运行（初次见面）不算你的任务；形象工坊用你的语言说「先登录」；NanoLika Cloud 0.16 可以把额度池设成任意数值，单个账号或全部（`/v1/admin/pool`、`/batch`），已经在生产环境。

### 升级说明

- **Android 覆盖安装 0.1.32，数据保留。** `NanoLika-0.1.33-arm64.apk`（Android 8.0+，arm64；versionCode 34）和之前每个版本用同一把签名。校验：`sha256sum -c NanoLika-0.1.33-arm64.apk.sha256`。
- **桌面端从这一版开始会记住。** 0.1.33 第一次启动会选一个端口写进 `~/.nanomuse/desktop/port`；0.1.32 存在当天随机端口名下的浏览器存储不会被带过来，引导页和 star 提示可能再出现一次。
- **`?token=` 链接对 0.1.33 的运行时不再有效。** 网页版和手机自 0.1.31 起就用首帧鉴权和 `#token=`；仍然拼 `?token=` 的脚本会收到一个说明改法的 401。
- **连接器。** 客户端 ID 这条路和浏览器交接都能编译并逐行照着桌面端的流程走，但维护者还没在真机上登录过某个服务或完成过一次交接（构建机上没有模拟器），哪个服务走不通、是哪家厂商，是最有用的反馈。
- **iOS 目前是 TestFlight 内部测试。** Muse 外壳只在 iPhone 上；iPad 保留上游布局。还没有公开链接。
- **自己跑中继的话：** 0.16.0 增加 `POST /v1/admin/pool` 和 `/v1/admin/pool/batch`，事件 `pool.set` / `pool.set.many`；不需要迁移。
- **桌面安装包没有经过公证。** macOS 第一次在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里点「仍要打开」（或右键 → 「打开」），Windows 点「仍要运行」。每个文件旁边都有校验和。
