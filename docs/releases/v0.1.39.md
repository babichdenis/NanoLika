# NanoLika 0.1.39 · Keys

🐉 **NanoLika `0.1.39` “Keys”**: the allowance is a start, your own key or a plan you already pay for is the way on, and every client now says exactly what each gives you. Eighteen providers sit in one catalogue: Alibaba Cloud Bailian, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, Zhipu, SiliconFlow, Volcengine Ark, MiniMax, Anthropic, xAI, Groq, Mistral and the servers on your own computer, each marked with what its key covers: chat, the hands, pictures, clips. A ChatGPT plan signs in on every client and covers chat and the hands; a provider without image or video models leaves pictures and clips off, and the app tells you in one sentence who could draw them instead of failing.

A device now shows and syncs the conversations of the account that is signed in and nothing of an earlier one's: a phone handed on, a family tablet, a work laptop with a personal account on it keep each account's chats apart, hidden rather than deleted, never pushed under the wrong key. On Ubuntu the hands' clicks land again: the glow drawn around the screen had been taking them. On a Mac the Computer Use helper asks for its permissions under its own name and keeps them. On the iPhone and iPad the crash after onboarding is gone. The fourteen Android and six iOS languages beyond English and Chinese are complete. The Terminal edition is no more; the desktop app is the desktop.

## Highlights

- **Your own key, from a catalogue.** One file, `providers.json`, shared by the phones, the desktop, the web console and the relay: eighteen providers with their endpoints, key pages, default models and what a key there covers, `chat · screen · pictures · clips`. The spent-allowance card leads with the region's provider (Bailian on the mainland, one key for all four; OpenRouter then OpenAI elsewhere), folds the rest under *more providers*, takes the key on the spot or opens the provider form filled in, and links to where a key is handed out. [docs/own-key.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md) is the catalogue in prose; the relay's guidance carries it to older clients too.
- **Sign in with a plan you already pay for.** *Sign in with ChatGPT* on the phones (the app's own sign-in; Claude, Kimi and OpenRouter too), on the desktop and in the web console (the runtime's `nanomuse chatgpt login`, the same PKCE flow as Codex, chat and the hands' screen reading through the Codex backend). A ChatGPT plan does not draw pictures or clips, and every client says so. The honest line stands under the row: OpenAI's terms cover a ChatGPT plan inside OpenAI's own Codex, other apps have lost that access before (OpenCode, January 2026), and a key keeps working if it stops.
- **One sentence, never a raw error.** Pictures, clips and the hands pick only from providers with image, video or vision models; when none has it, the avatar studio, *Image & video models*, the hands settings and the web console show one line naming the providers that would (for your region, in your language) with a link to how. New `[image]` and `[video]` slots in the runtime's configuration let pictures come from one provider and chat from another.
- **Whose conversations a device shows.** A synced conversation remembers the account that first pushed or pulled it. Sign in as someone else on the same phone or computer and the earlier account's chats leave the lists: they stay on the device, hidden, and are never pushed under the new key; the pull starts over for the new account; signing back in brings them back; signed out, everything local shows. Android, iPhone, the desktop and the runtime behind the web console follow the same rule (contract C10 in [docs/cloud.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/cloud.md)).
- **Ubuntu: clicks that land.** The glow drawn around the screen while the hands work is an X window the size of the display with a one-pixel input shape; Chromium resets that shape to the whole window whenever the window's bounds change (on creation, on first map, after each `setBounds` and again when the X server's ConfigureNotify arrives) so the glow took the clicks: the hands' at the right coordinates, and the person's own after an approval card. The glow now steps aside for every pointer action, the shape is set again after every event that can clear it, and a watchdog restores it the moment a real pointer event reaches the glow. Measured in a nested X server with GNOME Shell at 3840×2160, scale 2: ten of ten hands clicks, seven of seven after an approval card (five of the hands', two of the person's), the person's clicks after a resize two of two where 0.1.38 had none of three.
- **macOS: Computer Use keeps its permissions.** The dmg's quarantine flag was inherited by the bundled *NanoLika Computer Use* helper, so macOS ran it from a translocated read-only path with a new identity on every launch: the Screen Recording pane never showed its row and the grant never stuck. The app clears the flag before the helper's first start, refuses a translocated copy and says to move NanoLika to Applications, restarts the helper once rather than twice, and reports the display scale correctly on Retina displays; `press` and `release` hold a key across the actions that follow.
- **iPhone and iPad: no crash after onboarding.** Build 9 died on an iPad every time the chat appeared: the chat screen's body was one chain of some sixty modifiers, 0.1.38 added four, and the Swift runtime's type decoder overflowed the main thread's stack instantiating the result. The chain is shorter than 0.1.37's now (grouped modifiers, the composer stack behind one `AnyView`), a test keeps it that way, and the app builds with Xcode 27.
- **Every string in every language.** The fourteen Android locales beyond English and Chinese carried thirty-seven NanoLika strings and showed 840 in English; six iOS languages carried about a hundred of 718. Both apps are complete now: German, Spanish, Filipino, French, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Thai, Turkish on Android; German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Traditional Chinese on iOS, and a test keeps the Android files at the same keys.
- **No Terminal edition.** The `nanomuse-desktop-terminal` packages are no longer built; the desktop app is the one desktop shape, and the Python runtime stays for self-hosting and the web console (`GET /api/providers` and `/api/chatgpt/*` are new there).
- **The README** shows its features as cards and the four clients around one account, and the project has a [Discord](https://discord.gg/bkTySmm28X).

## Upgrade Notes

- **Android installs over 0.1.38 and keeps your data.** `NanoLika-0.1.39-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0+, arm64; versionCode 40) is signed with the same key as every version before it. The sync store migrates in place on the first start (Room v3, an owner per conversation; conversations synced before 0.1.39 belong to the account they were synced with). Verify with `sha256sum -c NanoLika-0.1.39-arm64.apk.sha256`.
- **The relay is 0.21.** `GET /v1/me` → `spend.guidance` and the `allowance_exhausted` refusal carry the provider catalogue by region; 0.20 clients ignore the new fields, 0.1.39 clients fall back to their bundled catalogue on a 0.20 relay. Deploy the relay first so the first cards are complete: `cloud/deploy/…/deploy.sh` or `docker compose up -d --build`.
- **Desktop and runtime.** `[image]` / `[video]` in `config.toml` are optional, without them the chat provider's host draws, as before. `nanomuse chatgpt login|status|logout|proxy` are new; the callback lands on port 1455 of the machine the runtime runs on: a runtime on another machine takes the address the browser ended on, pasted into the web console's card, the CLI's prompt or `POST /api/chatgpt/callback`.
- **Linux desktop:** nothing to do; the X11 fix is in the app. Wayland is still told plainly that the hands are off.
- **macOS desktop:** install the dmg into Applications and open the app once (*Open Anyway*); the helper's quarantine flag is handled by the app, and the permission panes list *NanoLika Computer Use*. Ad-hoc signed still: a new build starts the grants over.
- **iPhone and iPad:** build 10 on TestFlight; the public link follows Apple's beta review. The single 0.1.38 sync table is re-filed under the account the first time it is seen signed in.
- **The desktop installers are not notarised yet.** On macOS open the app once from *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (or right-click → *Open*); on Windows click *Run anyway*. The checksum sits next to every file.

## Community

nanoMuse is open source and free. Sign in with a phone number or an e-mail and you get a starting allowance; the developer pays for it. The account page shows what is left and how to add more. When it is gone, use your own key or a plan you already pay for ([how](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)). With an account, the text of your main conversation is kept on nanoMuse Cloud so that your devices show the same chat (side chats only if you say so) and one switch in Data controls turns it off and deletes it; nothing is sold ([privacy policy](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/)); delete the account whenever you want. The same relay runs on a server of yours: [docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md).

### 亮点

- **自己的 key，从一份目录里选。** 一个文件 `providers.json`，手机、桌面端、网页控制台和中继共用：十八家服务商，各自的接口地址、申请 key 的页面、默认模型，以及这把 key 能做什么，`对话 · 屏幕 · 图片 · 视频`。额度用完的卡片先列你所在地区的那一家（大陆是百炼，一把 key 四样全有；海外先 OpenRouter 再 OpenAI），其余折在「更多服务商」里，key 当场填或打开已填好的服务商表单，旁边就是去哪申请。[docs/own-key.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md) 是这份目录的文字版；中继的引导信息也会把它带给旧客户端。
- **用你已经在付费的套餐登录。** 手机上「用 ChatGPT 登录」（应用自己的登录流程；Claude、Kimi、OpenRouter 也可以），桌面端和网页控制台走运行时的 `nanomuse chatgpt login`（和 Codex 一样的 PKCE 流程，通过 Codex 后端做对话和「手」的看屏）。ChatGPT 套餐不画图、不出视频，每个客户端都会说明。这一行下面是实话：OpenAI 的条款只覆盖在 OpenAI 自家 Codex 里使用 ChatGPT 套餐，别的应用曾被切断过（OpenCode，2026 年 1 月），停了的话换 key 照样能用。
- **一句话，不报原始错误。** 图片、视频和「手」只从有图像、视频或视觉模型的服务商里选；一个都没有时，形象工作室、「图像与视频模型」、「手」的设置和网页控制台都显示一行字，按你的地区和语言点名能做的服务商，并附上怎么做。运行时配置新增 `[image]`、`[video]` 两个槽位，图片可以来自一家、对话来自另一家。
- **一台设备显示谁的对话。** 一条已同步的对话记得最先推送或拉取它的账号。在同一台手机或电脑上换成别人登录，之前账号的聊天就从列表里退出，留在设备上，藏起来，绝不会推到新 key 下面；新账号从头拉取；换回来就都回来；退出登录时本机所有的都显示。Android、iPhone、桌面端和网页控制台背后的运行时遵守同一条规则（[docs/cloud.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/cloud.md) 里的契约 C10）。
- **Ubuntu：点得动了。** 「手」干活时屏幕四周的光晕是一个和屏幕一样大的 X 窗口，输入形状只有一个像素；窗口边界一变，创建时、首次映射时、每次 `setBounds` 之后、以及随后 X 服务器的 ConfigureNotify 到达时，Chromium 就把这个形状重置成整个窗口，于是光晕把点击吃掉了：「手」的点击坐标对了却落在光晕上，审批卡片之后人的鼠标也点不动。现在光晕在每次指针动作时先让开，每个可能清掉形状的事件之后都重新设置，再加一个看门狗：真实的指针事件一旦落到光晕上就立刻恢复。在嵌套 X 服务器里用 GNOME Shell、3840×2160、缩放 2 测过：「手」的点击十次十中，审批卡片之后七次七中（「手」的五次、人的两次），窗口尺寸改变后人的点击两次两中，0.1.38 是三次零中。

### 社区

nanoMuse 开源、免费。手机号或邮箱登录后有一份起始额度，钱是开发者出的；剩多少、怎么加，账号页里有数。用完可以换成自己的 key，或者用你已经在付费的套餐登录（[教程](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)）。登录后，主聊天的文字会保存在 nanoMuse Cloud，让你的几台设备看到同样的对话（旁聊只在你说要的时候才上去）「数据控制」里一个开关就能关掉并删除；数据不卖（[隐私政策](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/)）；账号想删就删。同一个中继也能跑在你自己的服务器上：[docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md)。

现在是预览版。我们自己每天在用，知道哪些地方还糙；哪里坏了、想要什么，直接提 issue。[路线图](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md)写着哪里需要帮手。谢谢每一位装过、试过、报过问题的人。[提 issue](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/new/choose) · [发 PR](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Discussions](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/discussions) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/bkTySmm28X) · [点个 Star](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse)，觉得有用，点个 Star，让更多人看到。
