# NanoLika 0.1.26 · Window

🐉 **NanoLika `0.1.26` “Window”** puts a phone in your browser where the web version was (a private Muse on it, DeepSeek behind it, your Cloud account in front of it) lays the desktop out the way Muse's is, and says plainly where the project stands.

The headline is **a window onto the real thing**. [nanomuse.cn/web](https://nanomuse.cn/web/) now opens on a simulated phone (MobileGym's) with nanoMuse on it: sign in to nanoMuse Cloud on the phone's own pages, the Android app's door, a code to a phone number or an inbox, or your password; a first sign-in creates the account, with its free allowance, so the app is already yours the day you install it, and a Muse of your own starts for two hours, talking with DeepSeek V4 Pro, with lines to try beside it. A card beside the phone and a line under the pill say what this is: a demo in a simulator, a long way from the Android app, and where the app is.

The other story is **a desktop that looks like one**. On a wide window the web app and the desktop app have a rail of icons with the agent on top, the chats in a column beside it while the chat is open, settings with their sections down the left (Muse's shape) plus a quick-chat shortcut, *Start with the computer*, and the developer side behind one switch. The README, in ten languages under `docs/readme/`, gains a word on where the project is: a preview with rough edges, apps for anyone, a product that keeps moving and settles as it goes, and what we are after, a personal agent that belongs to the person who runs it, built in the open, worked out with users and developers everywhere.

## Highlights

- **The phone in the browser takes the web version's place.** [nanomuse.cn/web](https://nanomuse.cn/web/) leads to the showcase: a simulated phone with a private Muse, started after a sign-in to nanoMuse Cloud (`POST /api/demo/signin/*`; the gateway keeps the account's opaque id and masked identifier only, revokes the key the relay issued, and hands the browser a ticket good for thirty days; one account is one person: `PER_ACCOUNT_ACTIVE`, `PER_ACCOUNT_DAILY`). The kept Muses per account (`WEB_ENABLED=1`) are off in production. `DEMO_SIGNIN_REQUIRED=0` keeps the old way for an operator who wants no sign-in.
- **DeepSeek V4 Pro answers in the showcase.** `MAIN_MODEL` defaults to `deepseek-v4-pro` on 阿里云百炼; Hands, which reads a screenshot at every step, falls back to the sighted `qwen3.8-27b` on the same key when the main model is a text-only family (`GUI_MODEL` still picks it by hand).
- **A demo is called a demo.** The card beside the phone (the phone and its apps are MobileGym's re-creations, the NanoLika in it the lite web app, a long way from the Android app) with *Get the Android app* and *Desktop* buttons; the same line under the pill on the phone; the Android app's notice card (free, open source, non-profit) on the welcome page; the meet page named after the app.
- **The desktop lays itself out the way Muse's does.** A rail of icons with the agent's face and name on top (a tap opens its activity, the approvals, what it may do and what comes next), the five sections and the devices (a green dot when another of yours is online); Account, Settings and a *More* menu (*Report a bug* with the version and platform filled in, *Developer tools*, the version) at the bottom. While the chat is open, the chats sit beside the rail: a search box, the *Main chat*, the *Side chats* with a +. Settings has its sections down the left.
- **Quick chat, start with the computer.** The desktop app's Settings → *Desktop app*: *Start with the computer* (Windows and macOS; opens in the tray), and the shortcuts, a quick chat on ⌥ Space (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+Space (Windows, Linux) brings the window up with the cursor in the composer, also in the tray menu; the window on ⌘⇧M / Ctrl+Shift+M; Stop on ⌘⇧Esc / Ctrl+Shift+Esc.
- **The developer side behind one switch.** Settings → *Developer* → *Developer tools* (this device only) puts the *Coding* screen in the rail and shows the runtime's address and where its token is, with links to the command line and to the DeepSeek Harness bundle; `#coding` still opens the screen.
- **The avatar studio is a screen on the web and the desktop, the phone's**: the face at the top cycling through its moods, *Describe a new look* with the seven style chips and *Draw four*, *Pick one* on a 2×2, the poses and the clips drawn in the background with a progress line; the ··· menu has *Name & style*, *Image model*, *Redraw the poses* and *Back to the built-in look*.
- **The README in ten languages, under `docs/readme/`,** with one README on the repository's front page, a header of two lines (the languages, six badges) and the new *Where we are* section before *Contribute*; the same words on the site, in the release-notes template and the roadmap.
- **The Linux desktop app works again.** Every request to the 0.1.25 Linux runtime died with `No module named 'tkinter'` (`pyautogui`'s `mouseinfo` exits the interpreter when Tk is missing); the runtime now registers a stand-in before the import, and a backend that fails any other way merely counts as unavailable.
- **Windows: a machine that intercepts its own loopback is told so, or served over `::1`.** A proxy client in TUN mode or security software that swallows local connections left `nanomuse serve` hanging forever; the runtime now waits three seconds, falls back to the IPv6 loopback, and otherwise stops with an explanation naming the usual culprits (`nanomuse/loopback.py`); the desktop shell makes the same test first.
- **A device keeps the face it drew when another device renames** (relay 0.7.1): the relay names the pictures it holds (`face_id`), a device whose worn face matches takes the name and nothing else, and the face's description and style travel with it.
- **Releases in the shape people know**: a short story, *Highlights*, *Upgrade Notes*, *Community*, then *What's Changed*, *New Contributors*, *Contributors* and the *Full Changelog* link generated from the merged pull requests (`scripts/release_notes.py`).
- **Commit subjects follow Conventional Commits**: `type(scope): subject` on every commit of a pull request, checked by CI; [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) has the types and the scopes.
- **nanoMuse on DeepSeek Harness: an internal preview, not in any build.** The start of the next desktop as a set of plugins on [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness): the account as the *nanoMuse Cloud* provider, the Muse style on the first run, Reach both ways and a task from the phone, `harness/dsh-nanomuse/`, [docs/harness.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/harness.md).

## Upgrade Notes

- **Android installs over 0.1.25 and keeps your data.** `NanoLika-0.1.26-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0+, arm64; versionCode 27) is signed with the same key as every version before it. Verify with `sha256sum -c NanoLika-0.1.26-arm64.apk.sha256`.
- **The desktop installers are not notarised.** On macOS open the app once from *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (or right-click → *Open*); on Windows click *Run anyway*. They arrive a little after the APK (CI builds them from the tag) and `SHA256SUMS-desktop-app.txt` / `SHA256SUMS-desktop-terminal.txt` list every checksum.
- **The web version at nanomuse.cn/web is now the phone in the browser.** The kept Muse per account that 0.1.23–0.1.25 served there is off; what you had in it (a demo workspace, not your account) is gone with it. Your account, its allowance, name and face are untouched and work in the apps; the showcase asks for the same sign-in.
- **The Coding screen moved behind a switch.** On the web and the desktop, Settings → *Developer* → *Developer tools* brings it back to the sidebar; `#coding` opens it either way.
- **Running the showcase?** `DEMO_SIGNIN_REQUIRED` is on by default and wants `WEB_RELAY_URL` (the relay the sign-in goes to) and `VISITOR_DB` on a volume; `MAIN_MODEL` now defaults to `deepseek-v4-pro`: set it back by hand if you want the old model. [demo/showcase/README.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/demo/showcase/README.md).
- **Where to get it.**

  | Windows 10+ | `NanoLika-Desktop-0.1.26-win-x64.exe` (the app) · `nanomuse-desktop-terminal-0.1.26-windows-x64-setup.exe` (terminal) |
  | macOS 12+ | `NanoLika-Desktop-0.1.26-mac-arm64.zip` / `-mac-x64.zip` (unzip, drag to Applications) or the `.dmg` (the app) · `nanomuse-desktop-terminal-0.1.26-macos-arm64.pkg` / `-x64.pkg` (terminal) |

  GitHub's own downloads are the fastest source from China too in our measurements; if they fail where you are, the same files are on [nanomuse.cn/dl/v0.1.26/](https://nanomuse.cn/dl/v0.1.26/) within fifteen minutes.

## Community

**Free, open source, non-profit: open source, built together: a personal agent for all.** Sign in with a phone number or an e-mail and the model comes with a free allowance, paid by the developer, the account page says how much is left and how it grows; when it is gone, bring your own key, with a step-by-step guide. Messages are not stored unless you choose to contribute them; nothing is sold; delete the account whenever you like.

This is a preview, and the people building it use it every day; what breaks for you, and what you wish it did, is the most useful thing you can send. What we are after is bigger than one app (a personal agent that belongs to the person who runs it, built in the open on infrastructure anyone can reuse) and we look forward to finding out how far it goes with users and developers everywhere. Thank you to everyone who tried a build, reported what broke and asked for what was missing, every issue, idea and pull request brings a personal agent within everyone's reach.
