# NanoLika 0.1.36 · Thread

🐉 **NanoLika `0.1.36` “Thread”** makes your conversations one thread across your devices. Signed in, the phone, the iPhone, the Mac and the PC show the same chats: what you asked on the bus is on the desktop when you sit down, and the answer the desktop wrote is on the phone when you leave. The text travels through NanoLika Cloud; files and images stay where they were made. And from any device you can hand work to another: start a message with `@Mac` and the Mac's Muse does it and reports back; the iPhone takes such tasks too while the app is open.

The other story is the desktop's hands. On a scaled display they clicked beside their targets, and their effects were poor. Both are gone, not by more trial and error of our own, but by taking the wheels ByteDance's UI-TARS-desktop has already proven: its operator (nut.js) now lives in the desktop app, screenshots come at the display's size, the model's coordinates are pixels of the picture it saw and are mapped exactly once, and the prediction marker turns at the point of every action. On a 4K display at scale 2 the click lands within a pixel.

The iPhone is brought level with the phone on the things that showed: a way back from voice to the keyboard, the pill composer and grey bubbles, a floating header, *Rename chat*, the missing settings rows. And the small things: the face takes the click on a Mac, the splash is the logo, Ubuntu shows the icons, the console saves the *Star asks* again.

## Highlights

- **One conversation on every device.** After each finished turn the text of the chat (what you wrote and the final answer, never tool steps, tool results or the system prompt) goes to NanoLika Cloud and comes back on every device signed into the account: pulled at launch, on return to the foreground, the moment another device pushed, and once a minute. All devices' main chats are one conversation; a chat deleted on one device is deleted on all; chats from another device carry *From Pixel 8*. Routines, goals, the feed and work done for another device stay on the device that ran them. *Settings → Data controls → Sync conversations between my devices* is on when you are signed in; *Delete synced conversations* empties the store; both act for the whole account. The relay keeps at most 20,000 messages per account and cuts a message at 16 KB; the operator sees counts, not text. On the desktop the other devices' chats appear under *From other devices* and *Continue here* makes a session of them.
- **Work on another device from the chat.** A message that starts with `@<device name>` (the full name or its first word, case-insensitive, an online device of the account) is handed to that device's Muse through the hub, and the answer comes back to this chat as a turn. Devices has *Ask this device*, which prefills the composer; the web composer offers the devices when you type `@`. Computers take work whenever they are online, phones while the app is open: the iPhone now answers such calls itself.
- **The desktop's hands are UI-TARS-desktop's.** The operator moved into the Electron main process (`@computer-use/nut-js`): screenshots through `desktopCapturer` at the display's size, move-then-click with a 100 ms rest, drag, scroll in each platform's unit, clipboard typing for non-ASCII text, the hotkey table (⌘ for `ctrl` on a Mac), served to the runtime over a per-launch loopback server with a random token. The runtime's rule is *the picture is the unit*: `computer_screen` says the picture's size, `computer_act` takes `x`/`y` or a `box` in it, the picture is snapped to Qwen's 28-pixel grid so the model's own resize is the identity, and one mapping turns picture pixels into the operator's screen. `[hands] coords = "norm1000"` for models that answer on a 0–1000 grid. The glow draws the prediction marker (a turning dashed ring and a dot at the exact point, the action's name beside it, a trail for drags) from the operator's own coordinates. A Wayland session says why there is no operator. Apache-2.0 attribution in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- **iPhone: back from voice, and the phone's chat.** The voice panel's header has a keyboard button that keeps the transcript and brings the keyboard back, and the tab bar hides while the panel is open. The composer is the phone's one-row pill (a bare `+` for camera, photos, files and commands; a bare mic that becomes the send arrow); your bubbles are flat grey; the main chat's header floats on a blur over the transcript and side chats get the same bar. *Rename chat* in the menu and on a long press in the drawer, *Delete chat* next to it. Settings gained *Background & notifications* and *Hands* in the phone's places; the Face ID row and the old settings' *NanoLika* entry are gone from the shell; the old Cloud welcome banner is deleted.
- **The face takes the click on a Mac.** The chat header is no longer a drag region with holes cut into it, nothing draggable lies under the face at all; one empty strip above the face moves the window, and on macOS the face sits below the title-bar band. Windows keeps the strip to the left of the caption buttons; full screen puts the header back.
- **A splash that is only the logo**: the app icon in a quiet ring, the wordmark, one status line; the start screen no longer reads anyone's face.
- **Icons on Ubuntu.** The GNOME grid showed gears: the Electron package shipped a single 1024 px icon, a size the `hicolor` index does not have, and the terminal package shared its name, one overwrote the other. The Electron package carries 16–512 px icons now; the terminal package is `nanomuse-desktop-terminal`, with its own icon and desktop entry and a lower-priority `nanomuse-desktop` alternative, so both can be installed at once.
- **A task sheet for a Mac.** `docs/tasks/mac-check-0.1.36.md` is written for an agent on a Mac: what to build, how to find the face click, the permission texts, the restart dialog, the capsule's focus, window mode's clicks and Retina mapping, the new operator's native addon and coordinate space, and how to send the fixes back.
- **Console.** The *Star asks* card saves again (typing enables *Save*); the operator's page shows sync as counts.

## Upgrade Notes

- **Android installs over 0.1.35 and keeps your data.** `NanoLika-0.1.36-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0+, arm64; versionCode 37) is signed with the same key as every version before it. Verify with `sha256sum -c NanoLika-0.1.36-arm64.apk.sha256`.
- **Your conversations start syncing when you are signed in.** The first launch of each device pushes the text of its chats and pulls the others'; from then on each finished turn travels. This is a change to what the relay keeps: before 0.1.36 it kept no conversation text unless *Help improve nanoMuse's AI models* was on; now the text of your chats lives there, readable by your devices only, for as long as you keep the switch on. *Settings → Data controls → Sync conversations between my devices* turns it off and deletes what is stored, on every app; deleting the account deletes it too. The [privacy policy](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/) says what is stored, exactly. If you run your own relay, Cloud 0.19.0 adds `/v1/sync/*`; an older relay simply has no sync.
- **`@<device>` is read at the start of a message only.** `@Mac tidy the downloads folder` goes to the Mac; `tidy the downloads folder @Mac` stays here. Only devices that are online are matched, so a name that is not matched is sent as text.
- **The desktop's hands drive differently.** `auto` picks the app's operator when it is there, `pyautogui` / `xdotool` otherwise; `[hands] backend = "desktop"` insists. The screenshots the model sees are snapped to multiples of 28 pixels; `[hands] coords = "pixels"` (default) or `"norm1000"`. On macOS the same two permissions as before (Accessibility and Screen Recording for *NanoLika Desktop*) are enough; nothing new to grant. On Wayland there is no operator, and the Hands card says so.
- **macOS: still untried on a Mac by the maintainer** (the build machine is Linux), the face click, the permission words, the restart dialog, the capsule, window mode and the new operator's native addon are on the Mac task sheet above; a report from a Mac decides what changes next.
- **iOS is TestFlight-internal.** Left from the alignment: on iOS 16–18 the transcript starts below the floating header's edge rather than scrolling under it.
- **The desktop installers are not notarised.** On macOS open the app once from *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (or right-click → *Open*); on Windows click *Run anyway*. The checksum sits next to every file.

## Community

nanoMuse is open source and free. Sign in with a phone number or an e-mail and you get a starting allowance; the developer pays for it. The account page shows what is left and how to add more. When it is gone, use your own key: Alibaba Cloud Bailian in mainland China, OpenRouter elsewhere ([how](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)). With an account, the text of your conversations is kept on nanoMuse Cloud so that your devices show the same chats, one switch in Data controls turns it off and deletes it; nothing is sold ([privacy policy](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/)); delete the account whenever you want.

🐉 **NanoLika `0.1.36`「Thread」**：把你的对话在几台设备之间连成一条线。登录后，手机、iPhone、Mac 和 PC 看到同样的聊天：在公交车上问的，坐到桌前时已经在桌面端；桌面端写出的回答，出门时已经在手机上。对话文字经 NanoLika Cloud 传递；文件和图片留在产生它们的设备上。在任何一台设备上都能把活交给另一台，消息以 `@Mac` 开头，Mac 上的 Muse 去做并回报；应用开着时，iPhone 也接这样的任务。

另一条线是桌面端的手。在有缩放的屏幕上它点不准，效果也差。两个都解决了，不是靠我们自己再试错，而是直接拿字节跳动 UI-TARS-desktop 已经跑通的轮子：它的操作器（nut.js）现在就在桌面应用里，截图按显示器的尺寸来，模型的坐标就是它看到的那张图片的像素、只换算一次，预测标记在每个动作的落点上转动。4K 屏、缩放 2 的情况下，点击误差在一个像素之内。

iPhone 在那些看得见的地方追平了手机：语音能回到键盘、胶囊输入框和灰色气泡、悬浮顶栏、「重命名聊天」、缺的设置行。还有那些小事：Mac 上点形象有反应了，启动页是 logo，Ubuntu 显示图标了，控制台的「Star 提醒」能保存了。

### 亮点

- **每台设备上都是同一份对话。** 每轮结束后，聊天的文字，你写的和最终的回答，从不包括工具步骤、工具返回和系统提示，发到 NanoLika Cloud，再回到登录同一账号的每台设备：启动时、回到前台时、别的设备刚推送时、每分钟一次。所有设备的主聊天是同一个对话；一台上删掉的聊天，所有设备都删；来自别的设备的聊天标着「来自 Pixel 8」。例行任务、目标、动态和替别的设备干的活留在跑它们的那台设备上。「设置 → 数据控制 → 在我的设备之间同步对话」登录后默认开启；「删除已同步的对话」清空云端；两者都对整个账号生效。中继每个账号最多保存 20,000 条消息，单条截到 16 KB；运维者只看到数量，看不到文字。桌面端上别的设备的聊天在「来自其他设备」下，「在这里继续」把它变成一个会话。
- **在聊天里让另一台设备干活。** 消息以 `@<设备名>` 开头，全名或它的第一个词，不分大小写，必须是账号里在线的设备，就通过 hub 交给那台设备的 Muse，回答作为一轮回到这个聊天。设备页有「交给这台设备」，会预填输入框；网页版输入 `@` 时会列出设备。电脑在线就能接活，手机要应用开着，iPhone 现在自己应答这样的调用。
- **桌面端的手就是 UI-TARS-desktop 的。** 操作器搬进 Electron 主进程（`@computer-use/nut-js`）：用 `desktopCapturer` 按显示器尺寸截图、先移动再停 100 ms 再点、拖拽、按各平台单位滚动、非 ASCII 文本走剪贴板、热键表（Mac 上 `ctrl` 变 ⌘），通过每次启动随机 token 的回环服务提供给运行时。运行时的规则是「图片即单位」：`computer_screen` 说出图片的尺寸，`computer_act` 用这张图片里的 `x`/`y` 或 `box`，图片按 Qwen 的 28 像素网格对齐、让模型自己的缩放成为恒等，一次映射把图片像素变成操作器的屏幕坐标。`[hands] coords = "norm1000"` 给用 0–1000 网格回答的模型。发光层按操作器自己的坐标画出预测标记，落点上转动的虚线环和圆点、旁边的动作名、拖拽的轨迹。Wayland 会话会说明为什么没有操作器。Apache-2.0 署名在 `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`。
- **iPhone：从语音回来，以及手机的聊天。** 语音面板顶部有一个键盘按钮，保留转写、唤回键盘；面板打开时隐藏 Tab 栏。输入框是手机的单行胶囊（裸的 `+` 放相机、相册、文件和命令；裸的麦克风变成发送箭头）；你的气泡是平的灰色；主聊天的顶栏在毛玻璃上悬浮于对话之上，侧聊用同样的栏。菜单里和抽屉长按有「重命名聊天」，旁边是「删除聊天」。设置按手机的位置多了「后台与通知」和「Hands」；Face ID 行和旧设置里的「NanoLika」入口从壳里去掉；旧的 Cloud 欢迎横幅删了。
- **Mac 上点形象有反应了。** 聊天顶栏不再是「整条拖拽区再挖洞」，形象下面根本没有可拖拽的东西；形象上方一条空的拖拽条负责移动窗口，macOS 上形象位于标题栏带的下方。Windows 保留标题按钮左侧的拖拽条；全屏时顶栏回到原位。
- **只有 logo 的启动页**，安静圆环里的应用图标、字标、一行状态；启动画面不再读取任何人的形象。
- **Ubuntu 上的图标。** GNOME 的应用网格显示齿轮：Electron 包只带了一张 1024 px 的图标（`hicolor` 索引里没有这个尺寸），终端包又和它同名，互相覆盖。Electron 包现在带 16–512 px 的整套图标；终端包改名 `nanomuse-desktop-terminal`，有自己的图标和桌面项，并以较低优先级提供 `nanomuse-desktop` 这个命令，两者可以同时安装。
- **一份 Mac 任务单。** `docs/tasks/mac-check-0.1.36.md` 是写给 Mac 上的智能体的：构建什么、怎么定位点形象的问题、权限文案、重启对话框、胶囊的焦点、窗口模式的点击和 Retina 映射、新操作器的原生模块和坐标空间，以及怎么把修复发回来。
- **控制台。** 「Star 提醒」卡片能保存了（一输入就启用「保存」）；运维者页面以数量显示同步情况。

### 升级说明

- **Android 覆盖安装 0.1.35，数据保留。** `NanoLika-0.1.36-arm64.apk`（Android 8.0+，arm64；versionCode 37）和之前每个版本用同一把签名。校验：`sha256sum -c NanoLika-0.1.36-arm64.apk.sha256`。
- **登录后你的对话开始同步。** 每台设备第一次启动时推送自己聊天的文字、拉取其他设备的；之后每轮结束就传。这改变了中继保存的内容：0.1.36 之前，除非开着「帮助改进 nanoMuse 的 AI 模型」，中继不保存任何对话文字；现在只要开关开着，你的对话文字就在那里，只有你的设备能读。每个端的「设置 → 数据控制 → 在我的设备之间同步对话」可以关掉并删除已保存的内容；删除账号也会删掉它们。[隐私政策](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/)写了具体保存什么。自己跑中继的话，Cloud 0.19.0 新增 `/v1/sync/*`；旧中继只是没有同步。
- **`@<设备>` 只认消息开头。** `@Mac 整理一下下载文件夹` 交给 Mac；`整理一下下载文件夹 @Mac` 留在本机。只匹配在线的设备，没匹配上的名字按普通文字发送。
- **桌面端的手换了驱动方式。** `auto` 在有应用操作器时选它，否则 `pyautogui` / `xdotool`；`[hands] backend = "desktop"` 可以指定。模型看到的截图对齐到 28 像素的倍数；`[hands] coords = "pixels"`（默认）或 `"norm1000"`。macOS 上还是原来那两项权限（给 *NanoLika Desktop* 的辅助功能和屏幕录制），不需要新授权。Wayland 上没有操作器，Hands 卡片会说明。
- **macOS：维护者还没在 Mac 上试过**（构建机是 Linux），点形象、权限文案、重启对话框、胶囊、窗口模式和新操作器的原生模块都在上面的 Mac 任务单里；来自 Mac 的反馈决定下一步改什么。
- **iOS 目前是 TestFlight 内部测试。** 对齐还剩一处：iOS 16–18 上对话列表从悬浮顶栏的下沿开始，没有从顶栏下面滚过。
- **桌面安装包没有经过公证。** macOS 第一次在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里点「仍要打开」（或右键 → 「打开」），Windows 点「仍要运行」。每个文件旁边都有校验值。

### 社区
