# Pricing a plan from the provider's bill

English | [中文](pricing.zh.md)

## Summary

The relay bills an account in whatever currency the operator set, while the provider bills the
operator in dollars or yuan. This page is the arithmetic between the two: one multiplier over
the provider's list price, the assumptions it rests on, and what each plan therefore buys. It is
a record for the audit, not a shipped feature: the accounts and prices themselves are the
admin contour's, and nothing here changes a figure the relay charges.

Numbers are as of 2026-10-20. Provider prices move, so re-read the sources before quoting any
of them to a person.

## The multiplier

```
price to the person = provider cost × (1 + VAT / 100) × markup
```

- `VAT` defaults to **6** and is a provision, not something the provider charges: DeepSeek bills
  in dollars and adds no Russian tax. The operator collects it in the price and owes it
  themselves, so rounding it up is the safe direction.
- `markup` is the operator's own figure. **2.0** ("two shoulders") is what the arithmetic below
  assumes, and it lands as a 50 percent gross margin.
- Together: **×2.12** over the provider's list price.

Currencies:

| Assumption | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| dollar bought at | **100 ₽** | the operator's own purchase rate, held regardless of the market |
| dollar to yuan | 7.1 | the relay's `USD_CNY` setting, used for providers that bill in yuan |

An operator who changes either rate changes every figure on this page, and nothing else.

## What the provider charges

DeepSeek's own rates, read from
[api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing) on
2026-10-20. Prices are per million tokens. Off-peak is half of peak: peak hours are 01:00 to
04:00 and 06:00 to 10:00 UTC on weekdays, which in Moscow is 04:00 to 07:00 and 09:00 to 13:00.
A person in Russia therefore pays the lower rate in the evening and at night, when they actually
use it.

| Per million tokens | Provider | To the person (×2.12) |
|---|---|---|
| flash, input, off-peak | $0.15 = 15 ₽ | **31.8 ₽** |
| flash, output, off-peak | $0.60 = 60 ₽ | **127.2 ₽** |
| flash, input, peak | $0.30 = 30 ₽ | 63.6 ₽ |
| flash, output, peak | $1.20 = 120 ₽ | 254.4 ₽ |
| flash, cached input, off-peak | $0.003 = 0.3 ₽ | 0.64 ₽ |
| pro, input, off-peak | $0.66 = 66 ₽ | 139.9 ₽ |
| pro, output, off-peak | $1.98 = 198 ₽ | 419.8 ₽ |

Search and Fetch are billed separately at $0.75 per thousand successful requests, free for
server-side tool calls until 2026-11-01 (Beijing time). At the multiplier one search costs about
0.16 ₽; the relay's own free backends (`search.py`) cost nothing, so they stay the default.

## What one turn costs

A turn here is 3 000 prompt tokens of which 2 000 are cached, and 500 completion tokens, which
is what an ordinary chat exchange looks like with a system prompt and some history.

| | Provider | To the person |
|---|---|---|
| off-peak | 0.046 ₽ | **0.097 ₽** |
| peak | 0.091 ₽ | 0.193 ₽ |

| Plan | Turns off-peak | Turns at peak |
|---|---|---|
| 500 ₽ | ~5 200 | ~2 600 |
| 1 000 ₽ | ~10 300 | ~5 200 |
| 2 000 ₽ | ~20 700 | ~10 300 |
| 5 000 ₽ | ~51 700 | ~25 900 |

Half of a 500 ₽ plan is the provider's bill and half is margin. A plan also has to carry pictures
and clips, so the real mix is lower than this table; the account page should show what is left
rather than a number of turns.

## The trial pool, and where the risk actually is

A trial is cheap and the arithmetic says so. What costs money is not its size but the number of
people who take it and never pay.

| Trial | Costs the operator | Conversion needed at 500 ₽ and ×2 |
|---|---|---|
| 25 turns | 1.14 ₽ | 0.4 % |
| 50 turns | 2.28 ₽ | 0.9 % |
| 100 turns | **4.56 ₽** | **1.7 %** |
| 200 turns | 9.12 ₽ | 3.5 % |
| 500 turns | 22.80 ₽ | 8.6 % |

One paying person at 500 ₽ covers about 55 hundred-turn trials. **A hundred turns** is the
figure that keeps the required conversion under two percent, which is a rate a product like this
can reach. The remaining hazard is bulk sign-ups rather than generosity: the relay already
refuses disposable mail domains and gives one free pool per address and person
(`docs/cloud.md`, Allowance).

## Pictures and clips

These come from the provider the relay already speaks to, Alibaba Model Studio, whose rates are
recorded next to the menu in `cloud/nanomuse_cloud/config.py`. yuan at 7.1 to the dollar.

| Unit | Provider | To the person | Fits in 500 ₽ |
|---|---|---|---|
| one picture, qwen-image | ¥0.18 = 2.54 ₽ | **5.37 ₽** | 93 |
| five seconds, 480P | ¥0.50 = 7.04 ₽ | **14.93 ₽** | 33 |
| five seconds, 720P | ¥1.00 = 14.08 ₽ | **29.86 ₽** | 17 |
| five seconds, 1080P | ¥2.40 = 33.80 ₽ | 71.66 ₽ | 7 |

Two shoulders make 1080P clips a bad deal for a flat plan: either video carries a deeper
multiplier than chat, or clips are sold as packs of their own. That is a decision, not
arithmetic, and it is open.

## Not verified here

Prices of any provider other than the two above were not checked on 2026-10-20: their pricing
pages either render in JavaScript or refuse an automated request (openai.com answered 403,
bfl.ai served an empty shell, and the OpenRouter catalogue was blocked by the network policy).
Candidates worth pricing before an adapter is written:

- pictures: FLUX 3 and FLUX.2 (Black Forest Labs), gpt-image (OpenAI), Nano Banana and Imagen
  (Google), Seedream (ByteDance), Recraft
- clips: Kling 2.5 (Kuaishou), Hailuo 02 (MiniMax), Veo 3 (Google), Runway Gen-4, Sora 2
  (OpenAI), Wan 2.5 (Alibaba), Seedance (ByteDance)

Each one is an adapter as well as a price row: chat speaks the OpenAI protocol to the upstream,
while pictures and clips go through DashScope (`api.py`), and every other provider differs.

## Where the code already has room

Read only, for the audit: nothing below was changed by this page.

- `ModelSpec` in `cloud/nanomuse_cloud/config.py` carries `price_in`, `price_out`,
  `price_in_idle`, `price_out_idle`, `cached_in_rate`, `price_image`, `price_second` and the
  per-resolution table. These are the provider's prices, in yuan, per million tokens.
- `in_mult` and `out_mult` scale *charged tokens*, not money: a cheap model can stretch a grant
  without the person seeing a price.
- `allowance_cny` is the operator's bill ceiling per account (`/v1/config` reports it), and
  `uy` is micro-yuan, the integer unit of the ledger.
- A multiplier over the whole bill and the VAT at the top of it are the two figures this page
  adds, and they belong to the admin contour rather than to these fields.
