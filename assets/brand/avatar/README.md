# Bundled Lika

The website fairy replaces the built-in dragon artwork. `dragon` remains the profile ID;
all existing resource names and mood transitions retain their compatibility contracts.

Stills: 768 px WebP, generated with built-in imagegen from the approved website hero.
Clips: approximately twelve seconds, silent 512 px H.264 MP4, generated with MiniMax H3
through Higgsfield, then resized without cropping. Canonical assets are synchronised by
`scripts/sync-brand.py`. No live media service is needed to play bundled clips.

The full seated figure, shoes and wings fit the circle. The existing happy and error
status timing is unchanged. Error uses its still and the client's short reaction.
A person's custom studio face and own clips continue to take precedence.

## Prompt set

Shared still prompt: same Lika as the approved website hero, chestnut wavy bob, brown eyes,
sage leaf dress, coral scarf, translucent sage and amber wings, warm dimensional pencil
sketch. One full-body compact seated figure, both shoes and wings inside a circular safe
area, uniform ivory background, no labels, no border, no scenery, no cropped limbs.

- Idle: hands resting on lap, attentive gentle smile.
- Working: small laptop on lap, sage headphones, hands typing, focused eyes.
- Waiting: small warm crystal orb in her lap, expectant gaze.
- Happy: warm pleased smile, hugging a small amber star.
- Error: apologetic concerned expression, hand at temple, open other palm.

Shared video prompt: fixed camera and scale, preserve identity and full seated body,
no new objects or text, no speech or audio, start and end in the same pose and framing.
Idle breathes and blinks; working types, pauses and resumes; waiting looks at the person
and gently tilts her head; happy smiles, gives a subtle nod and settles back.
